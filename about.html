About Hengtai Raytech

Build here.
Keep it here.

We are a Nigerian defence industry partner building the technical depth, manufacturing capability and operational confidence that sovereignty requires.

Our position

We do not just deliver technology. We build capability that stays, grows and advances inside Nigeria.

Hengtai Raytech began as an equipment supplier and has repositioned as an original manufacturer. Our research and development partnerships are anchored inside Nigeria's defence industrial ecosystem, and our manufacturing footprint is domiciled within the country's military industrial complex.

That is a deliberate choice. It reflects a commitment to long-term Nigerian sovereign capability rather than short-term contract cycles.

What we commit to

Three pillars. One direction.

Every engagement is measured by what Nigeria can operate, maintain and develop after delivery.

01Manufacture

Local manufacture

Platforms including unmanned aerial systems, quadruped robotics and SAR payload integration are produced and assembled in Nigeria.

02Transfer

Technology transfer

Active international research partnerships create a route to joint R&D, co-development and knowledge that remains with local teams.

03People

Workforce development

Nigerian engineers, technicians and operators sit at the centre of our delivery model, with training built into every engagement.

The DICON alignment

From procurement to production.

The Defence Industries Corporation of Nigeria exists to build a national defence industrial base that reduces import dependency and anchors sovereign military production within the country's borders. Hengtai Raytech's direction is aligned with that mandate.
