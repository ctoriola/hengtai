Frequently asked questions

Ask the team
What does Hengtai Raytech build?

Airborne and orbital SAR sensing (MiniSAR and SmartSat-X), secure data links to ground control, and autonomous ground platforms (the X30 and M20).

Where are the systems made?

Unmanned aerial systems, quadruped robotics and SAR payload integration are produced and assembled in Nigeria, within the country's military industrial complex.

Which drones does MiniSAR work with?

MiniSAR mounts on standard drone interfaces and is compatible with DJI, ALLTECH and custom drone systems.

How is mission data protected?

Data is streamed in real time with AES256, military-grade encryption, and delivered geo-referenced to ground control stations and command centres.

Do you train our people?

Yes. Nigerian engineers, technicians and operators sit at the centre of our delivery model, with training and technology transfer built into every engagement.
